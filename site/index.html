Free, open-source PCB design

Lay it out. Play it. Order it.

PCBPro is a PCB designer for guitar pedals, amps and everything else. Lay out the board, see it in 3D, run the circuit and play your guitar through it before you build it, then order the boards and the parts.

Guitar pedals

From an empty box to a drilled pedal

Pick a Hammond enclosure and your knobs, and PCBPro shapes the board for the box, places the pots, adds the power and bias sections and the wire pads. When the circuit is done, it drills the box from the board.

  • Nine Hammond boxes, with their cavities, depths, drafts and screw bosses
  • Pots hang under the board, so every drill coordinate is mirrored for you
  • A 1:1 drill template, Tayda coordinates and a build sheet grouped the way you shop
  • Checks that the board, parts, knobs and jacks all fit and clear each other

The Amp Designer

Describe the amp. Get the whole build.

Choose guitar or bass, tube or solid state, a voicing from Blackface clean to five-stage extreme high gain, a power stage from 5 to 100 W and the features you want. The designer assembles a proven circuit and works out everything else.

  • Operating points for every tube and every B+ node, and the transformers to buy
  • The gain of every stage and where the volume knob starts to break up
  • A first power-up checklist and a voltage chart for every tube pin
  • A placed, routed board with high-voltage spacing from IPC-2221B

Amp boards built like amp boards

Tubes stand along the rear edge in their sockets, drawn in 3D with glass, plates and glowing heaters. Controls sit on the front edge. Each stage is packed between its tube and its knobs, and the supply is at the far end from the input.

  • Design rules from each net's voltage: pads and tracks spaced pair by pair
  • Checks for capacitor and resistor ratings, bleeders and tube wiring
  • A tone stack designer with real pot tapers, and 24 tubes with their pinouts

Simulation

Press Run and the board comes alive

LEDs light and blink, copper is tinted by its voltage, overstressed parts start to smoke, and buttons, switches and pots work. Double-click a net to put it on the oscilloscope. It's all PCBPro's own solver, so there's nothing else to install.

  • Analog parts, op-amps, 555s, regulators, logic chips and 220 real parts by number
  • Vacuum tubes with Koren's models, calibrated to their datasheets
  • ATmega and ATtiny chips running your real compiled firmware, with a serial monitor
  • As built mode follows the copper, so a missing track really leaves the LED dark

The editor

Everything a board needs

A layout editor that works from the board: place parts, connect their pads, route by hand or let the autorouter do it, pour the ground, and check it all.

{{video|autoroute||The autorouter}}

Route by hand, or don't

  • A 45° interactive router that shows clearance problems before you make them
  • An autorouter with vias, layer directions and pour nets stubbed in last
  • Copper pours with thermal reliefs, priorities and island removal, refilled after every edit
  • Net classes, and a design rule check you can click through

Under the hood

Engineering, not guesswork

Footprints are checked against datasheets, Gerbers against an independent parser, tube models against their datasheets, and the real-time audio model against the offline simulator. About 1,850 tests run on every change.

Get PCBPro

Up and running in a minute

The Windows download is a folder you unzip and run: no installer, no account. Or run it from source anywhere Python runs.

Windows

Download the latest release, unzip it anywhere and double-click PCBPro.exe.

Download for Windows

From source

Python 3.10 or newer. PCBPro.bat on Windows, ./pcbpro.sh on macOS and Linux.

git clone https://github.com/gjnail/pcbpro.git
cd pcbpro
./pcbpro.sh

PCBPro is free. If it saved you a board, a box or a blown fuse and you'd like to say thanks, you can buy me a coffee.
